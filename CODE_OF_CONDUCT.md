# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps this community:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people new to hardware hacking
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Showing empathy toward other members

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the
project maintainer through a private message on GitHub. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will
be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other maintainers.
